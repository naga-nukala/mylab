Net outflow = EMI + pre-payment + running costs − rent received (after vacancy). Appreciation compounds yearly; running costs and rent escalate at their own rates. Pre-payments apply at year-end and reduce principal (EMI stays the same, so the loan closes earlier). "Cumulative" starts with the one-time stamp duty + registration fee, then adds each year's net outflow.
