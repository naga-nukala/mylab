What do the fields mean?
  • Spend/mo: amount paid through this card each month.
  • Points % of spend: points earned per ₹100 of spend (1% = 1 point per ₹100).
  • ₹ per point: rupee value when redeemed (for example ₹0.25). Rupees = points × this value.
  • CASA %: interest on the savings account that funds this card.
  • Float days: days your money stays in the account before the bill is paid (about 30–45).
  • Redeem fee / every: one-time fee (about ₹49) spread over the months between redemptions (points expire in about 36).
