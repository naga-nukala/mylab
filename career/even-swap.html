Even Swap

Compare alternatives across objectives, then trade off one objective for another until a clear winner emerges.

How this works
  1. List your alternatives (e.g. job offers) and the objectives you care about (salary, growth, culture...).
  2. Fill in the table with how each alternative does on each objective.
  3. Look for a dominated alternative (worse on everything) — remove it.
  4. Pick an objective. Adjust its values so all alternatives are equal on it, compensating by adjusting another objective. Then hit "Neutralize" on that objective — it no longer affects the decision.
  5. Log each swap so you remember your reasoning. Repeat until one alternative wins or objectives run out.
